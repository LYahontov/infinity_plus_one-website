Home / Products / System for analytics decks to lenders

Lender-ready analytics decks, assembled from the source data.

A credit analytics company reports to the country's largest banks. Each deck took about 80 hours by hand. Now the first draft builds from the data in minutes, and revisions apply across the whole deck.

80 h → ~8 h

Time per deck

≈10× faster

End to end

30+ decks, ~10 yrs

Built on

minutes

Draft from Excel

01

The problem

Every report began as an Excel workbook. Someone rebuilt it by hand into 40–60 slides with up to ~290 charts, then spent a week on revisions: the same fix on twenty slides, five to twelve versions per deck.

02

The product decision

Don't automate slides, automate the source. Templates were derived from 30+ finished decks, so every chart and table is generated from the workbook, and every revision goes to the source and flows to every slide.

03

What we built

Excel in, branded deck out: charts, tables, takeaways, group legends · revisions by plain-language command or by notes on the PDF · diagrams drawn in Excel moved to slides one to one · PDF for the client and editable PPTX.

04

What changed

About 10× faster end to end. The analyst checks the story the numbers tell instead of retyping them, and slides can no longer drift out of sync with the workbook.

How it went.

~10 years of decks built by hand

Step 1

Step 2

Templates and revision engine

Today

Home / Products / Sovereign AI inference

Idle GPU servers, turned into a pay-per-token AI service.

Companies pick an open model for each task and pay only for the tokens they use — no subscription, and their data never leaves the jurisdiction.

0% → 80%

GPU load for the owner

1 week

From start to launch

10+

Companies using it

6

Open models

01

The problem — two sides

The owner had GPU servers standing idle: a cost with no revenue. Companies wanted LLMs but couldn't send sensitive data to foreign clouds, and didn't want a subscription for occasional tasks.

02

The product decision

Don't rent out servers, sell tokens. A catalogue of open models, each priced per token: clients pick the right model per task, the owner fills the GPUs with many small loads.

03

What we built

Model catalogue and one API · per-token billing, no subscription · isolation that keeps data inside the jurisdiction · load balancing across the owner's servers.

04

What changed

Idle hardware became a revenue line at 80% load. Clients got LLMs for sensitive data without moving it abroad, and pay only for what they use.
